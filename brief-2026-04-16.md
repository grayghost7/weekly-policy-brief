## 4. Emerging Questions

1. **Regulatory capture in AI child-safety laws**: When industry actors (OpenAI) covertly fund age-verification advocacy, what disclosure regime — lobbying law, FTC deception, or state transparency statutes — adequately addresses astroturfed "safety" coalitions?
2. **Bankruptcy as a privacy loophole**: Does existing consent doctrine (contractual, HIPAA, GINA) survive Chapter 11 asset sales of biometric/genetic data, and should Congress create a fiduciary-style "data trust" obligation that follows the data rather than the corporation?
3. **Kinetic risk to AI infrastructure**: What is the legal framework — insurance, force majeure, national-security designation, CFIUS-style review — for frontier AI datacenters located in geopolitically contested jurisdictions (UAE, Bahrain)?
4. **Executive defiance of structural statutes**: If the President attempts to nullify the Presidential Records Act or commandeer state election administration via spending threats, what non-impeachment judicial remedies remain functional given current SCOTUS composition?
